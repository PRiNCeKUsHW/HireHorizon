About HireHorizon

Where Ideas Find Their Horizon.

HireHorizon is a text-first social blogging platform. It borrows the rhythm of a social feed — follow people, like, reply, repost — and pairs it with room to write something longer than a status update.

Text only, on purpose

There are no image uploads anywhere on HireHorizon: not on posts, not on comments, not on profiles. Avatars are drawn from your initials. That keeps the focus on what people actually wrote, and it removes an entire class of moderation and security problems along the way.

What you can do

  • Write posts with a title and as much body text as you need.
  • Follow people and read a feed built from who you follow.
  • Like, reply, repost, bookmark and mention with @username.
  • Message other members privately.
